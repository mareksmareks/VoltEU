Why VoltEU

Your supplier sees these numbers.
Now you can too.

Spot-priced electricity contracts are common across Europe — especially in the Nordics, Germany, and the Baltics. Your supplier buys on the wholesale market and charges you at that price, often with a small margin on top.

VoltEU pulls that same data — published hourly by ENTSO-E, the European grid operator — and puts it in a format you can actually use: run your dishwasher at 3 AM when it costs a fifth of what it costs at 6 PM, or charge your EV before the morning peak.

01
Today & tomorrow, 24 hours each
Day-ahead prices publish at 13:00 CET. VoltEU fetches them the moment they're available so you can plan before the day starts.
02
1-hour and 15-minute resolution
Standard hourly view or granular 15-minute intervals where available. Set your own low and high price thresholds.
03
Your currency, your VAT rate
EUR, SEK, NOK, PLN, CZK, HUF and more — with accurate local VAT rates you can toggle on or off.
04
Auto-updates, no app store needed
The app checks for new versions automatically. One tap to install — no Google Play account required.
